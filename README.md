📌 Problem Statement
The organization is experiencing a high employee attrition (resignation) rate. The HR department lacks clear data visualizations to identify the true root causes, making retention strategies difficult to target and risking wasted costs.

🎯 Objectives
1. Measure the overall attrition rate and by segmentation (department, job role, demographics).

2. Identify the main factors correlated with attrition (overtime, job satisfaction, income).

3. Provide data-driven action recommendations for HR retention strategies.

🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
- Power BI Desktop: Data visualization & dashboard design.

- Power Query: Data cleaning, removing irrelevant columns, binning (Age Group, Income Band, Tenure Band).

- DAX: Creating measures (Total Employees, Total Attrition, Attrition Rate, Avg Monthly Income).

📈 Dashboard Pages
1. Overview: Key KPIs, attrition rate by department & gender.

2. Attrition Drivers: Analysis of Overtime, Salary, and Tenure.

3. Demographics: Employee profile (Age, Gender, Marital Status, Education).

4. Insights & Recommendations: Actionable recommendations for HR.

💡 Key Insights
- Employees who frequently work Overtime have an attrition rate ~3x higher.

- New employees (0-2 years) and those with Single status show the highest risk of leaving.

- Low Income earners and the 18-29 age group dominate the resignation rate.
